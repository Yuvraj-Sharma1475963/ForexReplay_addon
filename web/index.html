        Account settings

        1 lot = 100,000 units, so a pip is $10 per lot. Sizes are in steps of 0.01 lots. Saved in this browser.

        Session settings

        Each session is defined in its own city's clock, so it stays correct when that city changes its clocks. If the end is not after the start, the session runs past midnight.

        Load a preset:
        OnNameClockStartEndIndia timeColour

        Backtests

        Each replay with a trade or a drawing is saved as you go, with its trades, its drawings and where it got to. Resume rebuilds it by repeating every action on the same candles, then carries on live from there. Closed trades are also added to the journal (strategies/<journal>/trades.csv).

        Indicators

        Worked out from the candles on the chart only, so during a replay they never use a hidden candle. The last value moves while a candle is forming. They show on both charts, each on its own timeframe.

        simple moving average of the closes
        exponential moving average: recent closes count more
        volume-weighted average price, from 17:00 New York each day (tick volume)
        relative strength index (Wilder), in a panel under the chart, lines at 70 and 30
        average true range (Wilder) in pips, on the chart's own timeframe, in a panel under the chart
        average daily range in pips (the last completed days, never today), shown in the legend. (today's low + ADR and high − ADR, as the day develops; not on D1)

        Smart Money Concepts

        Order blocks and fair value gaps, ported from Smart Money Concepts by LuxAlgo (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, non-commercial). A block appears on the candle that breaks structure and goes when price mitigates it; a gap goes when price fills it.

        from the internal structure (5-candle pivots), the latest ones not yet mitigated
        from the swing structure, pivots of candles (known that many candles later)
        on the chart's timeframe, extended that many candles. (skip gaps from small candles)
        Mitigation
        Order block filter a candle twice as long as this is not used as an order block

        Fibonacci levels

        0 is where the move ended (your second click), 1 where it started. Below 0 and above 1 are extensions: −0.27 or −0.618 go beyond the end of the move, 1.272 or 1.618 beyond its start. Untick a level (or press ×) to leave it out.
